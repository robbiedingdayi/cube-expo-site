Who we are

One team from first sketch to final handover.

Since 2013 — Booth design · Fabrication · Global install

Shanghai Cube Expo Service Co., Ltd. manages every phase of your exhibition booth — from initial concept and design to fabrication and on-site installation. Based in Shanghai with a global reach, we work across industries and exhibition formats, bringing hands-on expertise and close attention to detail to every project.

Our process starts with understanding your goals, then moves through material selection, structural planning and build — keeping quality consistent at every step. Whether you are presenting at a major trade fair or a specialised industry event, we build booths that reflect your brand accurately, function well on a live show floor, and make an impression on the people you are there to meet.
